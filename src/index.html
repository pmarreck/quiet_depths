HEADPHONES RECOMMENDED · SAVES ON THIS DEVICE
 THE CAPTAIN’S FIELD GUIDE

Be hard to find.

01 / Listen

Passive sonar gives you a bearing, not a location. The chart draws an uncertain line. An active ping estimates range, but alerts nearby hunters. Contact estimates age; they are never a live enemy map.

02 / Take a look

At 15 meters or less, raise the scope. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom. Looking identifies ships and estimates range. Photograph merchant silhouettes with C. Fishing boats are neutral. A scope and a surfaced hull can be spotted.

03 / Stay alive

Electric is quiet; diesel needs 14 meters or less and recharges air and battery. R limits speed to 3 knots. Deep water muffles you, but 220 meters is test depth. Chart contours show reefs; keep water under your keel.

04 / Leave quietly

Select a contact to fire a homing torpedo. It seeks a loud target in its forward cone and may choose badly. Escorts hunt noise and drop charges. Turn, dive, release a noisemaker. Damage control slowly patches the hull and flooding at a battery cost.

05 / Borrow another pair of eyes

Deploy your inspection drone above 80 meters. W/S move, A/D turn, Q/E descend/ascend. Drag to look. Return it before its battery runs out. Your boat keeps its orders while you’re away.

06 / A patrol before bed

Choose 5× or 20× for travel. New contacts and attacks return you to 1×. Click a chart waypoint; ordered depth stays yours to manage. Return to the green extraction circle after your task. Space pauses; progress autosaves locally.
