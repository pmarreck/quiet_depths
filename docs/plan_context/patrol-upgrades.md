# Patrol upgrades

The normal patrol should preserve limited information on both sides. Enemy units should follow their own last observation of the boat, including observed heading and speed. They can predict from it, miss, lose the trail, and reacquire if the boat exposes itself. Depth charges and gunfire should become less accurate over distance or as a report ages. The existing three noisemakers are the starting point for a finite acoustic decoy feature.

After the patrol, give the player a short debrief about what gave them away, how the enemy searched after losing contact, and which evasive moves worked. Keep this information hidden during play. The owner endorsed learning from the completed patrol.

Difficulty belongs to each new patrol. Include damage advantage, detection sensitivity, ping detection chance with distance falloff, depth’s effect on detection, pursuit persistence, enemy air support, classified imagery, the ROV, chart omniscience, and orbit-camera availability. Regular chart contacts show uncertainty. Omniscience is an explicit option. The optional orbit view defaults off, hides the piloting UI, and keeps the patrol running unless the player pauses it. It needs drag and zoom on desktop and mobile and accessible pause and exit controls. Escape returns to the prior view on desktop. Keyboard remapping and reset are browser-stored preferences shared across patrols.

Keep the convoy objective and add a submarine patrol with a chance of an extra enemy submarine. Surviving and extracting is success, even with zero kills. Record commendations for enemy submarines sunk, a two-submarine achievement for a single patrol, and lifetime ship and submarine kills in browser storage. Keep the convoy’s submarine encounter probabilistic. Separate submarine tracks from surface tracks, and treat depth, course, and speed as estimates.

Make the game installable on mobile without a store download. Provide platform-appropriate install help, offline assets after the first complete load, and keep saved patrols when the installation updates.

Add sparse rising bubbles as motion references. Improve weapon readability through bubbles, shallow torpedo wakes, charge visuals, and forceful detonations from applicable views. Surface-wave lighting on the hull is desirable where it fits the rendering budget.

Use the patrol’s sky clock for mess sounds around 07:00, 12:00, and 18:00 and occasional period-style music from 19:00 to 23:00. Add short crew reactions for hits and leaks. Suppress or quiet onboard sounds while detected or silent running. Start muted as before. Any recorded audio assets should be cleared for redistribution with the game. The owner mentioned OpenAI and ElevenLabs as optional sources.
